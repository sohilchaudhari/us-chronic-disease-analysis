# U.S. Chronic Disease Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes the U.S. Chronic Disease Indicators (CDI) dataset from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC). The goal was to explore chronic disease patterns across the United States and apply machine learning techniques to classify and predict health outcomes.

## Dataset
Source: CDC U.S. Chronic Disease Indicators (CDI)

The dataset contains:
- 115 chronic disease indicators
- State-level health data
- Demographic and risk factor information
- Multiple years of public health records

## Methods Used
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Linear Regression
- Logistic Regression
- K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)
- Naive Bayes
- Support Vector Machines (SVM)
- Random Forest

## Key Findings
- Chronic diseases such as COPD, Cancer, Chronic Kidney Disease, Diabetes, and Cardiovascular Disease showed higher burden compared to other conditions.
- KNN and SVM achieved the highest classification accuracy.
- Random Forest identified confidence interval variables as important predictors.
- Data-driven approaches can support chronic disease surveillance and public health decision-making.
